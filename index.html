About

I am a social science researcher with a background in Political Studies and hands-on experience in survey research, field coordination and policy analysis. At Election Lab, an academic think tank at SUST, I co-design empirical research on electoral governance and citizen behaviour and coordinate primary data operations, from questionnaire design and enumerator training to data cleaning and statistical analysis.

My work moves between elections and public health. I have coordinated national and local election surveys, and I write on primary health care, climate anxiety, women's mental health and children's learning using DHS and MICS data.
