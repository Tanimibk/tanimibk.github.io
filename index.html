About

I am a social science researcher with a background in Political Studies and hands-on experience in survey research, field coordination and policy analysis. At Election Lab, an academic think tank at SUST, I co-design empirical research on electoral governance and citizen behaviour and coordinate primary data operations, from survey design to field logistics and statistical analysis.
